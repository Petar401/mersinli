What to drink with it

Şalgam and ayran: the drinks that go with tantuni.

One is sour, spicy and purple. The other is cold, salty yoghurt. Both are £1.50, and both are exactly what they drink with tantuni in Mersin.

By Mersinli Tantuni by ChobanUpdated September 20262 min read

In Mersin, tantuni comes with şalgam. It's the drink you'll see on almost every table at a tantuni counter. If you want something milder, the other classic is ayran. Here's what they are.

Şalgam: sour, spicy and purple

Şalgam (say shal-gam) is a fermented drink made mainly from purple carrots, with salt and bulgur, and sometimes turnip. It comes from the south of Turkey, around Adana and Mersin, where it's drunk ice cold with rich, spicy food.

It tastes sour, salty and a little like pickle juice, and it comes in two versions: acılı (spicy) and acısız (not spicy). It cuts through the richness of tantuni perfectly. The first sip surprises most people. By the third they're usually converted.

AllergensŞalgam is usually fermented with bulgur, so it contains gluten (wheat).

Ayran: cold, salty yoghurt

Ayran (say eye-ran) is yoghurt whisked with cold water and a pinch of salt. It's light, cooling and a little tangy, and in Turkey it goes with almost everything: kebabs, pide, gözleme and, of course, tantuni. If you like chilli, ayran is the drink that puts the fire out.

AllergensAyran is made from yoghurt, so it contains milk.
