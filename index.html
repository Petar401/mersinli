A bit about the dish

What is Mersin tantuni?

Tantuni is a hot wrap from Mersin, a port city on the Mediterranean coast of Turkey. The meat is chopped into small pieces and simmered first. Then it goes onto the sac, a wide shallow steel pan, with chilli flakes and a little oil. We spoon it into thin lavash with tomato, onions in sumac and a handful of parsley, and roll it up tight.

People in Mersin have been eating tantuni from street stalls since the 1940s. They still eat it the same way. Hot, quick, with a squeeze of lemon, some pickled peppers and a cold glass of şalgam.

  1. 01

    Simmered, then fried

    We poach the chopped meat until it's tender, then finish it on the hot sac so the edges crisp up.

  2. 02

    Rolled in lavash

    The lavash gets wiped across the pan to pick up the juices before we fill it. That's why tantuni is never dry.

  3. 03

    Served like in Mersin

    Tomato, sumac onions and parsley go inside. Lemon and pickled peppers come on the side.

What to drink with it

In Mersin, tantuni comes with şalgam.

Şalgam is a sour, spicy drink made from fermented purple carrots and turnip. If that sounds like a lot, try ayran instead. It's a cold, lightly salted yoghurt drink. All our drinks are £1.50.

Things people ask us

What is Mersin tantuni?

It's street food from Mersin in Turkey. Small pieces of meat are simmered, then fried on a flat pan with chilli flakes and rolled in thin lavash with tomato, sumac onions and parsley.

Where can I get tantuni in Norwich?

Come and see us at 1 Exeter Street, Norwich NR2 4QB. You can walk in or order online and pick it up.

How much does it cost?

Every tantuni is £6.50, whichever meat you pick. A gözleme is £4.50 and drinks are £1.50.

Is it the same as doner?

No. Doner is sliced off a turning spit. Tantuni is chopped small and cooked to order on a flat pan, so it's juicier, and it goes into thin lavash instead of pitta.

What should I drink with it?

Try şalgam if you like something sour and spicy, or ayran if you want something cool. We have both.
