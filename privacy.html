Privacy and cookies

This page explains what this website stores on your device, what it collects, and what choices you have. It was last updated in September 2026.

Who we are

Mersinli Tantuni by Choban, 1 Exeter Street, Norwich NR2 4QB. If you have a question about your data, ask us in the shop.

What we store on your device

Things this website stores in your browser
NameWhat it's forHow long
mt_basketRemembers what's in your order basket, so it's still there if you leave the page and come back. This is strictly necessary for the ordering page to work.Until you place the order or clear your browser data
mt_consentRemembers whether you allowed Google Maps, so we don't ask every time.Until you clear your browser data

Both are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and we don't use them to track you.

Google Maps

The map on our homepage comes from Google. Google sets its own cookies when the map loads, so we only load it if you choose "Allow Google Maps" or press "Show the map". You can read Google's privacy policy. The "Get directions" link opens Google Maps in a new tab, and it is up to you whether you use it.

Visitor statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count visits and check how fast our pages load. They don't use cookies and don't identify you. They only collect things like the page you viewed, the country, the type of device and browser, and page load times, all combined with everyone else's visits.

Online ordering

Online ordering is in preview. The name, phone number and notes you type at checkout stay in your browser and are not sent to us yet. When online ordering and card payment go live, we will update this page to explain how order details are handled.

  • Under UK data protection law you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
