100% halal meat

Halal food in Norwich, cooked the Mersin way.

All our chicken, beef and lamb is halal. Hot tantuni wraps and cheese gözleme, cooked to order at 1 Exeter Street.

Order for collectionFind us

Yes, all of our meat is halal. Every piece of chicken, beef and lamb we cook at Mersinli Tantuni by Choban is halal, and it has been since we opened on Exeter Street in 2024. There's no halal and non-halal version of anything on our menu: it is all the same.

What we serve

£6.50Chicken tantuni, halal
£6.50Beef and lamb tantuni, halal
£4.50Cheese gözleme, vegetarian
£1.50Şalgam, ayran and soft drinks

Our tantuni comes as a dürüm wrap or as a plate with salad, pickled peppers and lemon, with mild, medium or hot chilli. It's a hot, thin wrap from Mersin in southern Turkey. If you've never had it, here's what tantuni is.

Not eating meat? Our gözleme is filled with feta cheese, cheese and parsley, and has no meat in it.

How we cook

Every order is cooked fresh on the sac, a wide steel pan, when you order it. We cook in one small kitchen. Our chicken, beef and lamb are all halal, so there's nothing non-halal on the pans.

If you have a question about our meat or where it comes from, ask us at the counter. We're happy to talk about it.

AllergiesOur lavash and gözleme dough contain wheat (gluten), and gözleme and ayran contain milk. See the allergen table and tell us about any allergy before you order.

Quick answers

Is all your meat halal?

Yes. All our chicken, beef and lamb is halal.

Do you have vegetarian food?

Yes. The gözleme, with feta cheese, cheese and parsley, is vegetarian.

How much is a halal tantuni?

Every tantuni is £6.50, chicken or beef and lamb. Drinks are £1.50.

Can I order ahead?

Yes, you can build your order online and pick it up from Exeter Street.
