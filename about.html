Who we are

From Mersin to Exeter Street.

A small kitchen in Norwich cooking one of Turkey's great street foods the way it's made back home.

  • Quality
  • Trust
  • Flavour
  • 100% halal

Çoban means shepherd in Turkish. We picked the name because good tantuni starts with good meat, and a shepherd knows his flock. We opened on Exeter Street in 2024 to bring the taste of Mersin to Norwich, and we still cook every order fresh on the pan.

Why Mersin

Mersin is a city on the Mediterranean coast of southern Turkey, and it's the home of tantuni. People there take it seriously: the chop of the meat, the heat of the sac, the thinness of the lavash, the squeeze of lemon. We wanted to make it that way here, without shortcuts.

What we care about

QualityGood meat, fresh bread, and everything cooked when you order it.
TrustAll our meat is halal, and we're open about what's in our food.
FlavourThe way they make it back home in Mersin.

Mersinli Tantuni today

We keep the menu short on purpose. Two tantuni, chicken or beef and lamb, a cheese gözleme, and the drinks you'd have with them in Mersin. Everything is cooked on the sac in front of you.

Come and see us at 1 Exeter Street, Norwich NR2 4QB. We're open 10am to 10pm Sunday to Thursday, and 10am to 11pm on Friday and Saturday.
