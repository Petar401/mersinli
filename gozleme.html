Gözleme: thin, crisp, and full of cheese.

Feta cheese, cheese and parsley, folded into thin dough and cooked on the sac. Here's what gözleme is, and how we make ours in Norwich.

Order a gözleme

Gözleme is a thin, folded Turkish flatbread, filled and cooked on a hot sac until it's crisp outside and soft inside. It comes from village cooking all over Anatolia, where it has been made at home for a very long time, and today you'll find it everywhere from market stalls to motorway service stations in Turkey.

How gözleme is made

The dough is simple: flour, water and salt. It's rolled out very thin, traditionally with an oklava, a long and thin rolling pin. The filling goes on one half, the dough is folded over and sealed, and it goes onto the sac. It's turned once or twice and brushed with a little fat, then cut into pieces to serve.

Our gözleme

In Turkey you'll find gözleme with spinach, potato, minced meat and more. Ours is the cheese one: feta cheese, cheese and parsley, folded into thin dough and cooked on the sac until it's crisp. We serve it cut into four.

Quick answers

Is gözleme vegetarian?

Ours is. It's filled with feta cheese, cheese and parsley.

How do you say gözleme?

Roughly guhz-leh-meh. The ö sounds like the "u" in "fur".

Is gözleme like a crêpe?

Not really. The dough is thin, but it's a bread dough, folded around the filling and crisped on a hot pan.
